Why Aquatic Therapy Works — The Mechanisms

Up to 75%

Buoyancy

Water reduces gravitational load on joints — approximately 50% at waist depth and up to 75% at chest depth. This allows people to move freely who cannot move without pain on land.

12–14×

Resistance

Water provides 12–14x more resistance than air in every direction of movement — building strength simultaneously in opposing muscle groups without any joint impact.

360°

Hydrostatic Pressure

The gentle pressure of water against the body reduces swelling, improves circulation, and dampens the nervous system's pain response — therapeutic effects that begin the moment you submerge.

Who It's For

Built for the people who need it most.

The science is clear. Aquatic therapy works for every body.

Chronic Back Pain & Spinal Conditions

Patients with chronic low back pain were 6.6x more likely to achieve meaningful pain relief with aquatic therapy than standard physical therapy — and results held at 12 months. Disability reduction: 46.4% vs. 7%.
Peng et al. (2022), JAMA Network Open

Obesity & Weight-Bearing Limitations

Submerged to chest depth, the body offloads approximately 75% of its weight — reducing joint load to a level that makes sustained cardiovascular exercise possible for patients who cannot tolerate land-based activity.
ATRI / AEA Aquatic Therapy Clinical Guidelines

Chronic Pain & Fibromyalgia

Fibromyalgia patients who completed aquatic therapy reported half the pain of those who did land-based therapy — even six weeks after treatment ended. Pain scores: 2.7 vs. 5.5.
Rivas Neira et al. (2024), Physiotherapy

Post-Surgical Recovery

A study of 160 post-orthopedic surgery patients found water-based exercise produced significant improvements in pain, mood, and quality of life — concluded to be "an appropriate alternative strategy for post-orthopaedic surgical recovery."
Maccarone et al. (2022), Int. J. Biometeorology

Neurological Rehabilitation

Aquatic therapy produced significantly better outcomes in pain, balance, and gait than dry-land therapy for chronic stroke patients — across multiple clinical measures.
Pérez-de la Cruz (2020), Int. J. Environmental Research & Public Health

Athletic Injury Recovery

Deep water training allows patients recovering from ACL tears, rotator cuff injuries, and stress fractures to maintain cardiovascular conditioning and muscle engagement while completely offloading healing tissue — zero ground impact.
Established sports medicine protocol

Why Depth Is Everything

The Difference Between
Waist Deep and Chest Deep

TheraTank is the only pool that offers actual chest-deep water — 59" walls, 54"+ of usable depth, so even at 6 feet tall you're still at therapeutic depth. At waist depth, you offload 50% of your body weight; your hips, spine, and core are still loading — and for someone with chronic pain or post-surgical joints, that other 50% is still the problem. At chest depth, that offload reaches 75%, your joints barely register the load, and movement that felt impossible becomes available again.

That depth also makes true deep water running and suspension training with a buoyancy belt possible at home — workouts that used to require a lap pool or a clinic are now in your backyard.

How It's Made

Drop Stitch Technology.
Built Tough.

TheraTank is built from rugged, marine-grade PVC — the same material trusted in inflatable boats and whitewater gear. Drop stitch construction creates thick, rigid walls that don't flex, bow, or give under pressure. When inflated, it feels solid. Because it is.

That air chamber inside each wall does more than add strength — it acts as insulation, helping your water hold its temperature longer than a thin steel frame ever could. Less heat loss means less heating cost, and more time in the water.
